Travel Commerce

Travel commerce, built into the platform your members already use.

A points catalog or a gift card is a fine reward, until someone offers a week in Portugal instead. Travel is still the reward category people actually want, and most engagement, loyalty and community platforms hand that entire opportunity to an outside voucher vendor rather than owning it. We build the booking engine, the supplier connections, and the points-to-travel conversion logic that let your platform sell and service travel directly, under your own brand, without you having to become a travel company to do it.

The Opportunity

Travel is still the reward
members actually want.

Loyalty and engagement platforms compete on the perceived value of what members can redeem, and travel remains the category almost nobody in the ecosystem can deliver on their own. Techspian sits between the travel supply networks, GDS content, hotel and flight inventory, rate rules, and your platform's own experience, so travel becomes something members book inside your product, not something you send them elsewhere to find.

A New Revenue Line

Every hotel stay, flight, or trip booked inside your platform earns you a share of the commission, instead of that spend happening somewhere you have no relationship with at all.

Redemptions That Feel Bigger

Travel turns loyalty points and reward credits into something members value more than another catalog item, without you reworking your points economics to get there.

None of the Infrastructure Yourself

Supplier connections, rate aggregation, and GDS certification are a specialist's job. We carry that so your team stays focused on the platform, not on running a travel agency.

What We Build

Infrastructure most platforms
shouldn't build themselves.

Four pieces, built together or dropped in wherever your platform already has a gap.

White-Label Booking Engine

A search, booking, and itinerary experience that looks and behaves like it was always part of your platform, on web and mobile, not an iframe bolted on at the end.

Travel API & Supplier Connectivity

Direct integration with the GDSs, aggregators, and supplier networks your members' bookings actually need, Amadeus, Sabre, Expedia Partner Solutions, Hotelbeds, and more, unified behind one API so your team never has to reconcile five different supplier formats.

Points-to-Travel Redemption Engine

A conversion layer that turns loyalty points, reward credits, or member tokens into real travel purchasing power, with points-plus-cash splits built in wherever that lifts conversion.

Event & Group Travel

Lodging and group booking built straight into your conference or event registration flow, for retreats, masterminds, and incentive trips organized through your platform.
